# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure product quality through testing strategies, test automation, and validation of acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and quality standards for features and releases
- Coordinate manual and automated testing and maintain test coverage
- Validate acceptance criteria and report quality risks and defects
- Partner with Developers to investigate defects and improve testability

### Goals
- Find defects early and reduce production incidents
- Enable fast, confident releases through reliable quality signals
- Maintain clear ownership of testing and quality standards

### Success Criteria
- Acceptance criteria are validated before release
- Quality risks, defects, and test status are visible to the delivery team
- Automated tests provide timely feedback for supported workflows

### Typical Communication Patterns
- Acceptance-criteria reviews with Product Managers and Developers
- Test-status and release-readiness updates with Project Managers
- Defect triage and test-automation discussions with Developers

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
QA/Testing Leads work with Product Managers to clarify acceptance criteria, with Developers to build and maintain tests and resolve defects, and with Project Managers to communicate quality risks and release readiness.

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide architectural decisions, technical standards, and system design to keep solutions scalable and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design and architecture reviews
- Define and promote engineering standards and technical best practices
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and debt
- Mentor Developers and guide technical implementation decisions

### Goals
- Balance delivery needs with long-term system health
- Ensure systems are scalable, secure, and maintainable
- Build shared technical understanding across the team

### Success Criteria
- Significant technical decisions are documented and reviewed
- Architecture and technical risks are addressed before they block delivery
- Development work follows agreed technical standards

### Typical Communication Patterns
- Design reviews and architecture decision discussions with Developers
- Technical trade-off discussions with Product Managers
- Risk and dependency updates with Project Managers

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
Technical Leads and Architects support Developers with design direction and mentoring, help Product Managers assess technical trade-offs, and give Project Managers visibility into technical risks, dependencies, and delivery implications.

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate Agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach teams on effective Scrum practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify, track, and help remove team impediments
- Coach the team on agreed Agile and Scrum practices
- Use delivery metrics and retrospectives to drive continuous improvement

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability and sustainable team flow
- Foster continuous improvement and psychological safety
- Protect the team from avoidable interruptions

### Success Criteria
- Ceremonies result in clear plans, decisions, and follow-up actions
- Impediments are visible, owned, and resolved or escalated promptly
- Retrospective actions improve team processes over time

### Typical Communication Patterns
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies with the delivery team
- Impediment escalation with Project Managers
- Process-improvement discussions with Product Managers and Developers

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches help Developers and Product Managers collaborate effectively during delivery, coordinate with Project Managers on dependencies and escalations, and support the team in adopting agreed working practices.

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical implementation through requirements gathering, use-case documentation, and solution validation.

### Responsibilities
- Gather, analyze, and document business requirements and use cases
- Develop user stories and acceptance criteria with Product Managers and Developers
- Clarify requirements, assumptions, and business rules throughout delivery
- Validate that delivered solutions meet documented business needs

### Goals
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or incomplete requirements
- Ensure delivered solutions solve meaningful business problems
- Maintain a shared understanding of business needs and expected outcomes

### Success Criteria
- Requirements and use cases are clear, traceable, and agreed by relevant participants
- User stories include testable acceptance criteria
- Solution validation confirms that business needs are met

### Typical Communication Patterns
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- User-story refinement with Product Managers and Developers
- Requirements clarification and scope discussions with Project Managers

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
Business Analysts help Product Managers translate product goals into actionable requirements, work with Developers to clarify implementation details, and support Project Managers by surfacing scope assumptions, dependencies, and requirement changes.

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approvals, and governance to ensure project work remains strategically aligned.

### Responsibilities
- Provide strategic context, priorities, and business constraints
- Approve project scope, major changes, and key decisions
- Participate in milestone reviews and go/no-go decisions
- Remove organizational blockers and advocate for project outcomes

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy and priorities
- Enable timely decisions and effective governance

### Success Criteria
- Major decisions and approvals are provided within agreed timelines
- Milestone reviews confirm strategic alignment and business value
- Organizational blockers are resolved or escalated with clear ownership

### Typical Communication Patterns
- Project initiation, milestone reviews, and approval meetings
- Strategic updates with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Escalation and decision-making discussions when risks require sponsorship

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide Product Managers with strategic priorities and outcome feedback, give Project Managers approvals and support for escalations, and receive clear delivery and quality updates from the wider team, including Developers.
